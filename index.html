Posture Detection System Architecture

1. System Architecture

This system utilizes PoseNet with MobileNetV1 executed on TensorFlow.js WebGL to track 17 anatomical keypoints at 60 FPS with zero cloud data transmission.

2. Mathematical Posture Formulation

Angles are computed using standard trigonometric 2-argument arctangent:

Angle = atan2(y2 - y1, x2 - x1) * (180 / π)
  • Shoulder Alignment: Computed between left and right acromioclavicular joints.
  • Head Alignment & Tech-Neck: Angle between cervical spine (mid-shoulder) and cranial center.
  • Spine Angle: Vector from midpoint of hips to midpoint of shoulders.
  • Slouch Factor: Ratio of vertical distance between cranial baseline and shoulder plane.
